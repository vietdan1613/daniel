## 🧠 **How to Study Effectively**

To get the most out of this course, follow this 3-step study process:

**🔹 Step 1: Read the Blog**
Start by reading the companion blog posts for each topic. They explain concepts clearly and lay the groundwork for deeper understanding.

**🔹 Step 2: Watch the Video**
Next, watch Kent C. Dodds' YouTube videos or workshop recordings. These provide live demos and real-world context.

**🔹 Step 3: Code by Yourself**
Apply what you've learned by building the exercises on your own. Focus on solving problems without copying. This solidifies your skills and helps you internalize the patterns.
