The **Direct and Manage Project Work** process involves **leading and performing** the work defined in the **project management plan** to achieve the project's **objectives**. It includes **implementing approved changes**, **coordinating resources**, **managing stakeholders**, and **generating deliverables** while ensuring continuous **monitoring** and **adjustments** as needed.

This process is **ongoing** throughout the project and is essential to **ensuring project success** by **delivering outputs** that meet the agreed **scope**, **quality**, and **schedule** requirements.

---

## 🎯 **Purpose of Direct and Manage Project Work**

The purpose of this process is to:

- **Execute project activities** to meet project objectives.
- **Create deliverables** by following the project management plan.
- **Implement approved changes** (corrective, preventive, and defect repairs).
- **Monitor and control work progress** to ensure alignment with the project plan.
- **Manage project information** by capturing and sharing **work performance data**.
- Ensure that **issues and risks** are identified and **addressed** promptly.

---

## 🔑 **Key Inputs to Direct and Manage Project Work**

| **Input**                     | **Description**                                                       |
|--------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Project Management Plan**    | The **primary input** that outlines all planned project activities, processes, and controls. It guides the **execution** of project work. |
| **Project Documents**          | Documents such as the **requirements documentation**, **risk register**, **issue log**, and **stakeholder register** that are used to support project work. |
| **Approved Change Requests**   | Includes **corrective actions**, **preventive actions**, and **defect repairs** that have been formally approved and need to be implemented during project execution. |
| **Enterprise Environmental Factors (EEFs)** | External factors that influence the project, such as **organizational culture**, **regulations**, **market conditions**, and **available resources**. |
| **Organizational Process Assets (OPAs)** | Internal processes, **templates**, **lessons learned**, and **guidelines** that can assist in project execution. |

---

## 🛠 **Tools and Techniques for Direct and Manage Project Work**

### 1️⃣ **Expert Judgment**  
- **Project managers** and **subject matter experts (SMEs)** use their **knowledge** and **experience** to **make decisions** during project execution.  
- Expert judgment is used to determine how to **handle issues**, **implement changes**, and **allocate resources** effectively.

### 2️⃣ **Project Management Information System (PMIS)**  
- A **software tool** or system that helps the project team **track progress**, manage **resources**, and **share project information**.  
- PMIS tools include **scheduling tools**, **communication platforms**, **document management systems**, and **reporting tools**.

### 3️⃣ **Meetings**  
- **Meetings** are held to **coordinate activities**, **review progress**, and **address issues** as they arise.  
- Types of meetings include **status meetings**, **planning meetings**, and **problem-solving meetings**.

## 📤 **Outputs of Direct and Manage Project Work**

| **Output**                     | **Description**                                                       |
|--------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Deliverables**               | The **products**, **services**, or **results** created by the project. Deliverables must meet the agreed-upon requirements and specifications. |
| **Work Performance Data**      | Raw data collected during project execution, such as **work completed**, **hours logged**, **issues encountered**, and **budget spent**. This data is used for **monitoring and controlling** the project. |
| **Issue Log**                  | A document that tracks **issues** encountered during project execution, including their **status**, **impact**, and **resolution**. |
| **Change Requests**            | Requests for **corrective actions**, **preventive actions**, or **defect repairs** that arise during project execution. Change requests are submitted for **approval** through the **Perform Integrated Change Control** process. |
| **Project Management Plan Updates** | Updates to the **project management plan** as a result of **changes**, **issues**, or **lessons learned** during execution. |
| **Project Document Updates**   | Updates to project documents such as the **risk register**, **stakeholder register**, **schedule**, and **requirements documentation**. |
| **OPA Updates**                | Updates to **lessons learned**, **process documentation**, or **templates** based on the project team's experiences. |

## 🔄 **How Direct and Manage Project Work Relates to Other Processes**

| **Process**                      | **Relation**                                                        |
|----------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Develop Project Management Plan** | The **Direct and Manage Project Work** process uses the **project management plan** as a guide for project execution. |
| **Perform Integrated Change Control** | During execution, change requests may arise. These requests are processed through the **Perform Integrated Change Control** process for approval. |
| **Monitor and Control Project Work** | The data generated during project execution is used in the **Monitor and Control Project Work** process to track project performance and ensure that work is progressing as planned. |
| **Manage Stakeholder Engagement** | Stakeholders are engaged throughout project execution to ensure their **expectations are managed**, and **concerns** are addressed promptly. |

## 🔍 **Key Concepts to Remember**

| **Concept**                     | **Description**                                                       |
|----------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Deliverables**                 | The **end products** or **services** the project produces. Deliverables are created during project execution and are subject to **quality control** and **validation**. |
| **Work Performance Data**        | **Raw data** collected during project work, such as **percent completion**, **actual costs**, and **defects logged**. This data is later analyzed to create **work performance reports**. |
| **Change Requests**              | **Formal requests** to modify project documents, processes, or deliverables. They can include **corrective actions**, **preventive actions**, or **defect repairs**. |
| **Issue Log**                    | A document used to **track issues** encountered during the project, including their **impact**, **assigned owner**, and **resolution status**. |
| **Continuous Improvement**       | As work progresses, the project team should look for **opportunities** to improve **efficiency**, **reduce risks**, and **address problems** proactively. |

---

## 📝 **Common Types of Change Requests**

| **Type of Change Request**      | **Description**                                                       |
|---------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Corrective Action**           | Action taken to **realign** the project with the **project management plan** when a deviation occurs. |
| **Preventive Action**           | Action taken to **prevent** potential issues from occurring in the future. |
| **Defect Repair**               | Action taken to **fix a defect** in a deliverable or product that does not meet the project requirements. |

---

## 📌 **Summary**  
The **Direct and Manage Project Work** process focuses on **leading and performing** project work to produce the **deliverables** outlined in the project plan. It involves **implementing approved changes**, **managing resources**, and **monitoring performance** to ensure that the project remains aligned with its objectives. Outputs of this process include **deliverables**, **work performance data**, **issue logs**, and **change requests**. By effectively managing the work, the project team ensures that the project delivers the desired outcomes and **satisfies stakeholder expectations**.
