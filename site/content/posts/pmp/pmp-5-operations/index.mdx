## 📖 **What Is Operations in the Context of PMP?**
- **Operations** refers to **ongoing, repetitive activities** that produce the same output, such as manufacturing, service delivery, or maintenance.
- **Projects**, on the other hand, are **temporary endeavors** with a specific start and end, aimed at delivering a unique product, service, or result.

### 📌 **Differences Between Operations and Projects:**
| **Aspect**          | **Project**                           | **Operations**                       |
|---------------------|---------------------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| Purpose             | Temporary, unique outcome             | Ongoing, repetitive tasks            |
| Duration            | Has a start and end                   | Continuous                           |
| Focus               | Creating something new                | Maintaining and sustaining business  |
| Example             | Building a new website                | Running a customer support center    |
