**Enterprise Environmental Factors (EEFs)** are **internal and external influences** that impact a project’s success. These factors can **enhance or limit** project management options and must be taken into account by project managers when planning, executing, and controlling a project.

---

## 🧩 **Types of Enterprise Environmental Factors**

### **1️⃣ Internal EEFs (Within the Organization)**
These are factors that come from **inside the organization** and can affect the project. Internal EEFs are generally **within the control of the organization**.

Examples:
- **Organizational Culture** (e.g., values, mission, leadership style)
- **Company Structure** (e.g., functional, matrix, projectized)
- **Employee Skills and Experience** (e.g., technical, leadership, domain knowledge)
- **Infrastructure** (e.g., technology, facilities, equipment)
- **Resource Availability** (e.g., human resources, physical resources)
- **Information Systems** (e.g., project management software, ERP systems)
- **Work Authorization Systems** (e.g., policies for approving work)

---

### **2️⃣ External EEFs (Outside the Organization)**
These are **outside influences** that project managers have **little or no control over** but still impact the project.

Examples:
- **Government Regulations** (e.g., compliance requirements, laws)
- **Market Conditions** (e.g., competition, economic trends)
- **Industry Standards** (e.g., ISO, PMI standards)
- **Cultural Influences** (e.g., language, customs, business etiquette)
- **Political Environment** (e.g., stability, trade policies)
- **Legal Restrictions** (e.g., contracts, intellectual property)
- **Environmental Conditions** (e.g., weather, natural disasters)

---

## 📝 **How EEFs Impact Project Management**

EEFs influence project processes in the following ways:

| **Project Process Group** | **Impact of EEFs**                                        |
|---------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------|
| Initiating                 | Government regulations, market conditions                |
| Planning                   | Organizational culture, resource availability            |
| Executing                  | Employee skills, infrastructure                          |
| Monitoring & Controlling   | Information systems, reporting requirements              |
| Closing                    | Legal compliance, customer expectations                  |

---

## 🎯 **Key PMP Exam Tips:**

1. **EEFs are outside the control of the project team.**
   - Internal = Within the organization  
   - External = Outside the organization

2. **Differentiate EEFs from Organizational Process Assets (OPAs).**  
   - **EEFs** are constraints or influences.  
   - **OPAs** are tools or processes you can use (like templates, guidelines, historical data).
