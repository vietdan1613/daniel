The **Perform Qualitative Risk Analysis** process is used to **prioritize identified risks** based on their **probability** of occurring and their **impact** on project objectives. This process helps in determining which risks need **immediate attention** and which ones can be monitored with less effort. It is a **subjective, non-quantitative** process that provides an initial assessment of risks before performing more detailed **quantitative analysis** (if necessary). 

The purpose of this process is to assess the **severity** and **likelihood** of each identified risk and categorize them to allow for efficient allocation of resources in developing risk responses.

---

## 🎯 **Purpose of Perform Qualitative Risk Analysis**  
The purpose of this process is to:

- **Prioritize risks** for further analysis and response.
- **Assess the impact and probability** of risks in terms of how they may affect the project's objectives.
- **Categorize risks** into high, medium, or low priority to focus resources on the most significant risks.
- **Identify risk exposure** to determine the most critical areas of concern for the project.

---

## 🔑 **Key Inputs to Perform Qualitative Risk Analysis**

| **Input**                     | **Description**                                                       |
|--------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Risk Management Plan**       | Defines the **approach, methods**, and **tools** for conducting qualitative risk analysis. |
| **Risk Register**              | Contains the **identified risks**, including **descriptions**, **categories**, and **assigned owners**. |
| **Project Documents**          | Contains the **assumptions log**, **lessons learned**, and **stakeholder register**, which may provide additional context for assessing risks. |
| **Enterprise Environmental Factors (EEFs)** | Includes external factors that may influence the **probability** and **impact** of identified risks (e.g., **market conditions**, **laws**, **industry regulations**). |
| **Organizational Process Assets (OPAs)** | Provides historical data, **templates**, and **risk analysis criteria** from previous projects to help assess risks. |

---

## 🛠 **Tools and Techniques for Perform Qualitative Risk Analysis**

### 1️⃣ **Expert Judgment**  
- **Subject matter experts** and **experienced team members** are consulted to assess the **probability** and **impact** of risks, as well as their potential consequences. **Expert judgment** is particularly valuable when the data is insufficient or when there is a need for **experience-based evaluation**.

### 2️⃣ **Risk Probability and Impact Assessment**  
This technique involves evaluating **how likely** each risk is to occur and its **potential impact** on the project’s objectives (scope, schedule, cost, quality).

| **Assessment Criterion**      | **Description**                                                    |
|-------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Probability**                | The likelihood that the risk will occur (typically rated as **high, medium, low**). |
| **Impact**                     | The potential consequences if the risk does occur (typically rated as **high, medium, low**). |
| **Priority**                   | Combining probability and impact to establish overall **risk priority** (high, medium, low). |

### 3️⃣ **Risk Categorization**  
- Organizing risks into **categories** (e.g., **technical**, **external**, **organizational**, **project management-related**) to better understand where risks are most likely to occur and group similar risks together for easier analysis and response planning.

### 4️⃣ **Risk Urgency Assessment**  
- Determining the **urgency** of addressing risks based on their potential **timing** and **immediacy**. Some risks may require immediate attention, while others can be monitored over time.

### 5️⃣ **Expert Interviews**  
- Interviewing stakeholders, team members, or experts with **knowledge about the project** or its domain to gather their **opinions** and insights on the potential risks, their impacts, and their likelihood of occurring.

## 📤 **Outputs of Perform Qualitative Risk Analysis**

| **Output**                     | **Description**                                                       |
|---------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Risk Register Updates**      | The **Risk Register** is updated with the **results of the analysis**, including risk prioritization (based on probability and impact) and categorization. |
| **Risk Report**                | A report that summarizes the **results** of the **qualitative risk analysis**, which typically includes the **high-priority risks**, their **characteristics**, and **next steps**. |
| **Project Document Updates**   | Updates to **assumptions log**, **stakeholder register**, and **lessons learned register** based on new insights from the risk analysis. |

## 📋 **Risk Scoring and Probability/Impact Matrix**

| **Risk Scoring**  | **Impact**       | **Probability**   | **Priority**         |
|-------------------|------------------|-------------------|----------------------|
| **High**          | High (Critical)  | High (Likely)     | High Priority        |
| **Medium**        | Medium (Moderate)| Medium (Unlikely) | Medium Priority      |
| **Low**           | Low (Negligible) | Low (Rare)        | Low Priority         |

## 📊 **Example of Probability and Impact Matrix**

| **Impact \ Probability** | **Low** | **Medium** | **High** |
|--------------------------|---------|------------|----------|
| **Low**                  | Low     | Low        | Medium   |
| **Medium**               | Low     | Medium     | High     |
| **High**                 | Medium  | High       | Critical |

In this matrix, the **vertical axis** represents the **impact** of the risk (low, medium, high), and the **horizontal axis** represents the **probability** of the risk occurring (low, medium, high). This matrix helps to categorize and prioritize risks based on their severity and likelihood.

## 🔄 **How Perform Qualitative Risk Analysis Relates to Other Processes**

| **Process**                      | **Relation**                                                        |
|----------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Identify Risks**               | **Risk identification** is the first step, and the **Perform Qualitative Risk Analysis** process prioritizes the risks identified. |
| **Perform Quantitative Risk Analysis** | If risks have high priority, the **quantitative analysis** will follow to assess their **detailed impact**. |
| **Plan Risk Responses**          | After analyzing the **priority risks**, **response strategies** are developed based on their assessed **probability** and **impact**. |

---

## 📌 **Summary**  
The **Perform Qualitative Risk Analysis** process helps prioritize risks based on their **probability** of occurrence and **impact** on the project. It uses tools such as **risk probability and impact assessments**, **expert judgment**, and **risk categorization** to evaluate and rank risks. The **Risk Register** is updated with the results of the analysis, providing a clear view of the **most critical risks** that need immediate attention. This prioritization enables the project team to focus on the risks that could have the **greatest impact** on project success, making the **Plan Risk Responses** process more efficient and targeted.
